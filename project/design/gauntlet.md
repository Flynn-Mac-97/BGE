# Gauntlet: item synergies and builds

A builder agent changed the game; a fresh critic compared each version blind with the previous one and picked which one a
player would enjoy more. Fun was measured on nine signals with target bands (choices, synergy payoff, power spikes,
counterplay, close fights, pace, variety, fair death, collection). The scorer is a by-hand script and is not kept in the repo.

| Round | Change | Critic pick |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Enemy curve: more health, gentler damage growth; foes tire from a late cycle; offers favour new families; peddler stock set on arrival | kept (Fun 5.6 vs 3.3) |
| 2 | Lighter Heavy Blow and Swarm, heavier Plated and Regrowth, tire from cycle 14 | kept (Synergy 6.7 vs 0) |
| 3 | Bedroll heals only below half health; stronger Purifier and Regrowth | rejected: threats became less answerable |
| 4 | Items that work alone, Hunger fed by hits, Whetstone → Grindstone, new kits, strong-pair offers, a defensive offer before bosses | kept: deeper runs, distinct builds, no stalls |

The Build Lab climbs to floor 68, and the bot values cards over an eight-cycle bout, so items that stack or grow count.

## Open problems after round 4, ranked by the critic

1. Late stomps: 56% of fights are stomps (band ≤ 40%); deep Nettle bosses cost 3% HP.
2. Floor-10 Cellar Warden kills 4 of 10 Rook runs.
3. Kit items fill every run: Raw Meat in 4 of 5 Rook runs, Purifying Salt in nearly every Nettle run.
4. Only 20 of 44 runs reaching floor 15 evolve; Scholarship and Scavenging lack a growth item like the Grindstone.
5. Spiked and Swarm cause 11% and 13% of deaths with few answers around floors 20–25.

# Black Bell: the feeling

The feeling every Black Bell feature is tested against. Decided with the owner, October 2026, from the genre research in
`design/research/` and a moodboard of reference art.

## Statement

> With a candle, a pack of scavenged scraps and every potion I could keep, I went down into the old dark — and found strange
> ways to make those scraps enough.

## Pillars

1. **Every scrap counts.** Resources are limited, so every spend is a decision. At a shop, a chest or a level-up, the player
   weighs what they can afford against what their build needs, and works out the best buy within their limits.
2. **Simple pieces, creative depth.** Few rules, each readable at a glance, that combine into builds nobody planned.
3. **The dark punishes.** Death is common and costly. Fights play themselves, but a bad plan is never excused.
4. **Earned, not given.** Every upgrade is felt and valued, inside a run and between runs. Nothing comes in floods.
5. **The old book voice.** Ink, candlelight and one strange line per item, enemy and floor. The world is bigger than the
   player understands.

## The profile

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Player fantasy | A flexible explorer in a deep, retro, text-flavoured dark fantasy |
| Items | Bare scraps and gear scavenged on the way; each one precious and strange |
| Mood | Old-school grim: candlelight, ink and mystery. Warmth only at the tavern. |
| Credit for a win | Build and luck in equal parts; luck offers the pieces, the build decides |
| Death | Still pays toward permanent upgrades, at a much smaller scale; each permanent gain should feel earned |
| Run pace | Idle-friendly: fights play themselves; the challenge is the plan |
| During a fight | Small interventions at key moments (consumables) |
| Difficulty | Highly punishing, but fair |
| Peak moment | A plan comes together and works |
| The chase | Depth record, rare items, builds to discover, the world's story |
| Surprise | From drops and the stair itself, with creative interactions |
| The crew | Strange wanderers for hire; many come and go |
| Voice | Sparse but evocative text, not dialogue |
| Hub | A dark-fantasy tavern of hireable adventurers, with a crafting table |
| Power in a run | Humble start, hard-won strength |
| Other players | Solo and private |

## Moodboard notes

- Cross-hatched ink dungeons lit by one candle or torch (Darkest Dungeon fan art).
- Old fantasy book plates with short lore blurbs per creature (Vermis).
- Hand-painted found objects with one-line mysteries: rings, keys, lanterns, odd potions, a wooden doll.
- Glyphs the player cannot read: some things are never explained.
- Plain stat cards: HP, damage and speed, honest and readable.
- Odd, colourful wanderers, never polished heroes.

## Changes to plan (not yet made)

The balance work of October 2026 leaned toward abundance. Against these pillars, plan to review:

- **No item cap and the forced new-item card.** Abundant finds work against "every scrap counts".
- **30% lucky training and jackpot chests.** Keep the spikes, but make them rarer and more valued.
- **The planned stat shop.** Keep, but price it so each buy is a real trade-off, with no free spam.
- **Bells per run and the endless tower.** Shrink the scale so each permanent upgrade feels earned.
- **Item and enemy text.** Add one strange line to each item, enemy and floor.

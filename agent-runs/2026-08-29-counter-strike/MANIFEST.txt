agent-runs — the raw record of the Counter-Strike 1.6 build, kept for analysis.

Three rounds of parallel agents built a 3D FPS runtime and game on top of a 2D
sprite engine. These files are what the agents were asked, what they returned,
what independent reviewers found wrong, and what it cost. Nothing here is a
conclusion — it is the evidence.

briefs/round-1-foundation.mjs      the workflow script for round 1: renderer, physics,
                                   input, camera, textures, lighting. 6 lanes + 6 verifiers.
briefs/round-2-game-layer.mjs      round 2: weapons, damage, movement, bots, match rules,
                                   buy menu, HUD, effects, models, materials, lights.
                                   11 lanes + 11 verifiers.
briefs/round-3-guns-and-focus.mjs  round 3: weapon models in hands and on screen, and a
                                   full-screen play mode. 2 lanes + 2 verifiers.

builder-results.jsonl              one line per lane: files written, whether check and the
                                   test suite passed, the lane’s own summary, and its
                                   handover notes to the other lanes.

verifier-findings.jsonl            one line per defect an independent reviewer confirmed by
                                   reading the code. Fields: round, severity
                                   (blocker|major|minor), file, line, problem.

agent-cost.jsonl                   one line per agent: input, output, cache read and write
                                   tokens, turn and tool-use counts, the tool mix, and the
                                   largest single line in its transcript.

painpoints.snapshot.jsonl          a copy of painpoints.jsonl as it stood at the end of the
                                   run, so the friction entries stay matched to these
                                   findings. The live log is at the repo root.

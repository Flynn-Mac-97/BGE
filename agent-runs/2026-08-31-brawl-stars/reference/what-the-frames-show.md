# Brawl Stars, read off eight store frames

The frames are in this directory. They are Supercell's own store shots, so the
right-hand third of each is a marketing character over a blue band — read only
the arena, top-left.

What every gameplay frame does:

- **Camera.** Top-down, tilted about 55 to 60 degrees. The whole arena fits. A
  character is roughly a tenth of the frame height.
- **Heads.** A head is about 40% of the character's height. The body is small and
  the silhouette is head-first at any size.
- **Outline.** Every character carries a dark saturated outline, thick enough to
  survive against sand and against water.
- **Name plate.** Each actor wears its name and a green health bar above the
  head. Identity is interface, not silhouette alone.
- **Ground ring.** A soft coloured ellipse sits under each character. It is what
  separates an actor from the floor, and it is how a player finds their own.
- **Floor.** Warm sand in large flat blocks, one value, no grain. Props are
  chunky and sparse — cactus, barrel, bush, skull.
- **Boundary.** A hard edge of another colour, usually blue water, so the arena
  ends somewhere the eye can see.
- **Impact.** A small bright yellow star burst at the point of a hit. Small,
  saturated, gone quickly. Never a cloud, never a dark stain.
- **Colour.** Mid-tone warm ground, high-saturation characters on top of it, one
  cold colour reserved for the boundary.

The blind comparison a critic must make: put one of these beside one in-game
frame with the labels covered, and say which is the game and which is the
reference. If the answer is easy, name what gave it away.
